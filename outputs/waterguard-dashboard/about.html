01 · THE CHALLENGE

Water information is hard to act on.

Public monitoring reports contain valuable local measurements, but they are spread across studies, locations, and sample dates. NeerWatch organizes that evidence geographically and pairs it with clearly labeled prototype assessments so users can prioritize follow-up work.

02 · HOW IT WORKS

One workflow, three kinds of evidence.

01 / EVIDENCE

Explore published observations

Browse mapped locations, source periods, recorded water parameters, and cited references. Measurements stay tied to their historical samples.

02 / PROTOTYPE MODEL

Inspect risk and explanation

Model Studio displays saved zone predictions, verification-priority scores, data-reliability scores, and SHAP contributors for an assessment zone.

03 / VERIFICATION

Plan a current field check

Select a site, record a visit, and enter readings taken with a field kit. Present-day decisions still require current field and laboratory testing.

03 · THE MODEL

What the model output means.

Signals and explanation

The prototype schema represents water-quality measures, rainfall, historical case counts, source consistency, and sensor reliability. Saved prediction and SHAP records let a reviewer inspect a zone’s estimate and see which listed features pushed it higher or lower.

Current build: the repository contains prediction and explanation CSV snapshots dated August 2024. It does not include executable XGBoost weights or live sensor connections, so it cannot produce a new prediction for an uploaded row. “Data reliability” describes input quality; it is not model accuracy.

Designed for transparent review

  • Historical published samples are shown separately from synthetic model zones.
  • SHAP bars describe saved model contributions; they do not establish cause.
  • CSV upload checks required columns and row structure only.
  • No output is a current water-safety certificate.

05 · RESPONSIBLE USE

Decision support, with limits made visible.

NeerWatch is a demonstration platform. Historical observations describe past conditions at their sampled sites; the ML snapshot is synthetic and dated. Neither replaces a calibrated field visit, laboratory analysis, or public-health guidance.
